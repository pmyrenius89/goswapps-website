A LITTLE CLARITY

Website privacy.

This notice applies to the GoSWAPPS Studios website at goswapps.com. App privacy policies are separate from this website notice; it does not describe how our apps handle information.

A simple, static website

This website does not use analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, browser storage, contact forms, or embedded third-party content. Fonts and other website assets are served with the site. There are no accounts or newsletter sign-ups.

Visiting the website

Delivering a web page requires a hosting provider to receive technical request information, such as an IP address and browser information. If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process that information as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is separate from the website’s use of cookies or analytics.

Contacting us

Email links open your email application. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to handle your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive or unnecessary personal information.

Questions

For website privacy questions, email hello@goswapps.com. For app support, email support@goswapps.com.
